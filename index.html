How it goes

Four steps. No account managers, no discovery workshops.

  1. 01

    You describe it

    One message: what the business does and what the page has to achieve.

  2. 02

    I quote it

    A fixed price and a fixed list of sections, in writing. You approve before I start.

  3. 03

    You see it live

    A real URL within days, not a mockup. You request changes on the actual page.

  4. 04

    You own it

    Plain files and the domain in your name. A short call on how to update it.

I'm Hasan. I'm 14, I live in Kannur, and RYZE is just me — no studio, no subcontractors, no one to forward your email to.

I started building websites because I wanted the internet to feel less templated. Since then I've shipped 10+ sites for real clients — my own school's official site, an architecture firm, a retailer in Abu Dhabi — completed a frontend internship, and founded Tech Orbit Hub, a developer community that now spans a few countries.
